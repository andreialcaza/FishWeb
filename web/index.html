    Insane Aquarium Web Help

    The Basics

    Feed your fish
    Click or tap the tank to drop food. A pellet normally costs 5 coins. Hungry guppies turn green. Feed them to help them grow and keep them from starving.
    Collect coins
    Click or tap coins before they disappear. Larger guppies drop more valuable coins.
    Defend the tank
    Shoot aliens by clicking or tapping them. Aim at one side to push them the other way. To defeat Gus, drop pellets for him to eat.
    Finish a level
    Spend your coins at the shop along the top of the tank. Buy all three egg pieces to advance in Adventure or win a Challenge tank.

    With the tank focused, use the arrow keys to aim and press Space or Enter to act.

    New fish
    Buy fish such as guppies and carnivores from the shop. The selection varies by tank, and more slots open as your fish grow.
    Food quality
    Better pellets keep guppies and breeders full longer and help them grow faster.
    Food quantity
    Increase the number of pellets you can have in the tank at once.
    Laser upgrade
    Increase the damage of each shot so you can defeat most aliens in fewer hits.
    Unlock pets
    Completing an Adventure level unlocks a pet with its own ability.
    Itchy
    The swordfish chases and attacks aliens.
    Stinky
    The snail collects coins near the bottom of the tank.
    Choose your pets
    Adventure levels let you bring up to three pets. Once you have more than three, choose which ones to bring before the next level.
    Adventure
    Clear levels across several tanks and unlock pets. Your browser saves your level and pet choices. After reloading, Continue Adventure starts at the beginning of your saved level.
    Time Trial
    Finish with the highest balance you can before time runs out. Buy eggs to hatch extra pets.
    Challenge
    Buy three egg pieces while prices rise and alien waves grow. Each egg piece resets the other shop prices.
    Virtual Tank
    Spend shells to stock and decorate your own aquarium.
    Sandbox
    Choose More on the web main menu to open Sandbox. Hatch pets for free or use Sandbox Controls to add fish, pets, and aliens.
    Your aquarium
    Buy and name fish, feed them, and watch them grow over time.
    Shells
    Earn shells during play and spend them at the Virtual Tank store.
    Daily stock
    The store's selection changes with the real-world date.
    Rare finds
    Check the store on different days to find unusual fish and items.
    Feeding
    Feed your fish three times a day to keep them happy.
    Growth
    Fish that eat regularly grow in about a real-world week.
    Special diets
    Use the Feed button for fish that eat something other than pellets.
    Fish health
    Virtual Tank fish stay alive even if they miss meals.
    Fish
    View information about your fish, choose which ones to show, or sell them.
    Pets
    Choose which pets appear in your aquarium.
    Tank
    Pick a background and choose which decorations to display.
    Screensaver
    Open Tank and choose Screensaver to watch your aquarium in fullscreen.
